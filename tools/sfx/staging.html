Sound staging

  1. Click a variant to hear it. The one you clicked last is selected.
  2. Approve it, mark the sound Needs work, or say No sound here.
  3. Notes go to Claude. Say what to change: "shorter", "less bright", "B, but softer", "sounds like a microwave".
  4. Use In context to hear the sounds together, the way they'll follow each other in a game.
  5. When you're happy, ask Claude to promote the approved sounds into the game.

Variants of one sound are loudness-matched, so judge their character, not their volume. Levels between sounds are deliberate: card sounds are quiet, the Skitgubbe slam is loud. Try it on your phone too (serve.py --host 0.0.0.0).

In context

Short scripted moments of play. They use your approved variant, or the one you last clicked; sounds marked "No sound here" stay silent.
